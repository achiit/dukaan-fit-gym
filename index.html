RUN SMART • STAY SAFE

Run preparation
made simple.

A clear guide for before your run, after your run, and rest days.

Quick guide

Before running5–10 minEasy warm-up
After running5–10 minCool-down

Start gently. These are general suggestions, not a medical plan. Adjust to your fitness and comfort.

🔥

1. Before running

Dynamic warm-up · 5–10 minutes
1
Easy walk

Walk at a comfortable pace to warm up.

2 minutes
2
Ankle circles

Hold a support if needed; circle each ankle gently.

10 circles each direction per ankle
3
March in place

Lift knees comfortably; keep your posture tall.

30–45 seconds
4
Leg swings

Hold a wall or railing and swing one leg gently forward and back.

8–10 each leg
5
Easy jog

Begin slowly before moving toward your normal running pace.

2–3 minutes
Remember: Warm up with controlled movement. Do not force a stretch or bounce aggressively.
🧊

2. After running

Cool-down · 5–10 minutes
1
Slow to a walk

Reduce your pace gradually instead of stopping suddenly.

3–5 minutes
2
Calf stretch

Hands on a wall, one foot behind, heel down. Lean forward gently.

20–30 seconds each side
3
Front-thigh stretch

Hold a support, bend your knee and bring heel gently toward your body.

20–30 seconds each side
4
Hamstring stretch

Place one heel slightly forward and hinge at your hips with a straight back.

20–30 seconds each side
5
Easy breathing

Relax your shoulders and let your breathing settle naturally.

1 minute
Stretch gently: You should feel mild tension, not pain. Never bounce or force the position.
✅

💧

After-run recovery

Have water, and eat a normal balanced meal or snack when hungry. Include carbohydrates and protein across your meals. Give yourself rest days when needed and increase running gradually.

Safety first: Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, or unusually short of breath. If symptoms are severe or do not settle, get help from a trusted adult or medical professional.
